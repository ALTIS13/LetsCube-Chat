## Publication Ordering

The existing content lookup deliberately cannot authorize its eventual native
publisher. A final callback could otherwise race a replacement, confirmed read
or owner retirement. `ChatNotificationCleanup.updateCurrent` supplies only the
presentation-ordering part, under the existing card and owner locks:

- exact instance/owner/epoch lease, chat tag, protocol1, non-summary/id0,
  notification/message IDs and generation;
- exactly one matching active OS card, current process intent and a fresh exact
  durable intent read immediately before the synchronous publication;
- no generation allocation, routing mutation, callback retry or hidden ambiguous
  exception after the publisher may have had an effect;
- process-local preview retirement on an exact confirmed read, before querying
  or cancelling OS cards. Queued OS observation/cancellation failure cannot admit
  a late update, while existing pending cleanup/recheck behavior is retained.

The retirement bit belongs to the cached posted generation. A new generation is
not poisoned. It is not persisted content/credential authority: no asynchronous
callback survives process death, and a future cold reader still needs fresh
authenticated server authorization. The production presenter does not call this
new entry yet. Do not treat it as native credential or preview consent proof.
